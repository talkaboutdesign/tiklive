The dashboard is now a live view: chat with filters for followers, friends, minimum follower count and hidden words, highlighted gifts and subscriptions, a gifts column with top gifters, a way to silence someone in your own view, and an optional video preview. The raw event log is gone. `LiveUser` gains `followerCount`, `followingCount` and `followStatus` (`none`, `follower` or `friend`), and `SessionStatus` gains `hlsUrl`.
